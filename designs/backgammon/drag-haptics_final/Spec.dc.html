APP-964 · Backgammon

Drag a checker, feel the move

Drag is added next to tap-to-move, never instead of it (tap and screen readers keep working). Phone gets drag and haptics; the web keeps its drag and gets the same drop targets and illegal-move feedback, without haptics.

Gesture

  1. Press one of your checkers (the top of a stack, or the bar when you must enter). No long press.
  2. Lift after 6 pt of movement: the checker grows to 1.3×, gets a shadow and an orange ring, and rides 1.7 checker heights above the finger so the finger never hides it. The stack shows one fewer; legal points light up.
  3. Hover: the point under the lifted checker (not under the finger) is the target. A legal one brightens, glows, and shows a ghost where the checker will land. Illegal points stay unlit.
  4. Release on a lit point: the checker settles (spring, about 180 ms). A hit sends the blot to the bar (220 ms slide). Dropping on the tray bears off.
  5. Release elsewhere on the board: the checker springs back (220 ms ease-out) and the status line says why.
  6. Release on its own point or off the board: it springs back quietly. That is a cancel, not a mistake.
Combined moves (both dice) light up like single ones when “Combine two dice” is on. Reduced motion: no flights or spring, checkers place at once; haptics stay. An opponent move arriving mid-drag (online) cancels the drag and puts the checker back. The board never scrolls while a checker is lifted.
